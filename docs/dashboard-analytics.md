# The StoreSuite Dashboard — Your Store at a Glance

Log in, land on the dashboard, and within five seconds you know how your store is doing. That's the whole idea.

The dashboard home is the first thing you (and your team) see when opening StoreSuite. It greets you with *"Here's what's happening with your store today"* — and then actually shows you: sales numbers, a trend chart, your best sellers, the latest orders, and one-click shortcuts to the things you do most.

![StoreSuite Dashboard](dashboard-screenshot.png)

---

## Pick Your Time Window

Everything on the dashboard answers one question: *"How are we doing — compared to before?"* The date range picker at the top right decides what "before" means.

By default the dashboard shows **today** compared to the previous day. Click the picker and you can switch to any window you like — week to date, last month, last quarter, year to date, or a fully custom range — and choose whether to compare against the **previous period** or the **same period last year** (great for seasonal stores, where comparing December to November tells you nothing).

Change the range once, and every number, chart, and leaderboard on the page updates to match.

---

## Performance Boxes — the Numbers That Matter

The row of stat boxes across the top is your store's pulse. In the screenshot you can see:

- **Net sales** — revenue after refunds and discounts; the truest "how much did we make"
- **Orders** — how many orders came in
- **Average order value** — what a typical purchase is worth
- **Returns** — how much went back out as refunds
- **Net discount amount** — what your coupons and discounts cost you
- **Products sold** — units out the door

Each box also carries a small **percentage badge** showing how the number moved compared to your chosen comparison period — so you're never looking at a number in a vacuum.

And this row is yours to shape: in the plugin settings (**WooCommerce → StoreSuite → Dashboard** in the WordPress admin) you can switch on more boxes — total sales, gross sales, variations sold, taxes, shipping, downloads, and more — or hide the ones that don't apply to your store. Eight meaningful numbers beat fifteen noisy ones.

## The Net Sales Chart — Your Trend Line

Below the stat boxes, the **Net sales** chart turns your revenue into a picture. Two lines run across it: your **current period** and the **comparison period**, so spikes and dips have context. The legend underneath shows both totals side by side.

A few things you can do here:

- **Change the granularity** — the *"By day"* dropdown lets you group the data by day, week, or month, depending on how wide your date range is. Zoomed out to a year? Group by month and see the seasons. Looking at last week? By day shows you which weekday wins.
- **Switch chart styles** — toggle between a **line chart** (best for spotting trends) and a **bar chart** (best for comparing individual days) using the icons at the top right of the chart.
- **Hover anywhere** on the chart to see the exact figures for that point in time.

## Top Products — Your Leaderboard

The **Top products – Items sold** panel ranks your best sellers for the selected period, showing each product's **items sold** and the **net sales** it brought in. It's the fastest way to answer "what's actually selling?"

Depending on what you've enabled in the plugin settings, you can also show leaderboards for:

- **Top categories** — which sections of your store perform best
- **Top customers** — who's spending the most
- **Top coupons** — which promo codes actually get used

Like everything else on the page, the leaderboards follow your selected date range.

---

## Recent Orders — What Just Happened

The **Recent Orders** table shows your five latest orders: order number, customer (or *Guest*), date, total, and a colored **status badge** — yellow for on-hold, green for completed, and so on.

It's the "anything need my attention?" panel. A glance tells you if new orders are flowing in and whether any are stuck waiting. For the full picture — and to act on an order — head to **Orders** in the sidebar.

---

## Quick Actions — One Click to Everywhere

The **Quick actions** grid is your launchpad. Instead of navigating through menus, jump straight to:

- **All Products** — the product list
- **Add Product** — start a new product right away
- **View Orders** — the orders list
- **Create Order** — log a phone or in-person sale
- **Coupons** — manage your discount codes
- **Create Coupon** — spin up a new promotion
- **View Reports** — open the full Analytics section for deeper digging
- **Account** — edit your account details

If your day starts with "add the new arrivals" or "check this morning's orders," it now starts with one click.

---

## A Few Friendly Tips

- **Make the comparison work for you.** Running a promotion? Set the range to the promo week and compare with the previous period — the percentage badges instantly tell you if it moved the needle.
- **Seasonal store? Compare year-over-year.** December vs. November is misleading; December vs. last December is the truth.
- **Watch Returns alongside Net sales.** Climbing revenue with climbing returns is a different story than climbing revenue alone.
- **Use the dashboard as a morning ritual.** Date range "Today," ten seconds of scanning, and you know if it's a normal day or one that needs attention — before your coffee gets cold.
